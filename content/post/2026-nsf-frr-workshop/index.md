---
title: Prof. Guanrui Li Organized the Multi-Robot Autonomy Workshop at the 2026 NSF FRR Robotics Annual Meeting
date: 2026-09-26
---

Prof. Guanrui Li organized the Multi-Robot Autonomy Workshop at the [2026 NSF FRR Robotics Annual Meeting](https://nrifrr-mtg26.org/), held on September 25 and 26 in Pittsburgh. The workshop examined the future of multi-robot autonomy through the interplay of perception, mapping, learning, and planning.
