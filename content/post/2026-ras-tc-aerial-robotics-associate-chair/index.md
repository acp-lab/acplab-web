---
title: Prof. Guanrui Li Is Serving as an Associate Chair of the IEEE RAS Technical Committee on Aerial Robotics and UAVs
date: 2026-11-01
---

Prof. Guanrui Li has joined the leadership of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) Technical Committee on Aerial Robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles as an Associate Chair. The technical committee brings together researchers, practitioners, students, and industry working across aerial robotics, and organizes workshops and community events at major robotics conferences such as ICRA.

See the [technical committee's leadership page](https://tc-on-aerial-robotics.github.io/about/).
