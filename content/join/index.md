        The **Aerial-robot Control and Perception (ACP) Lab** at Worcester Polytechnic Institute is looking for people who want to make aerial robots agile, intelligent, and physically capable. Our research spans three pillars — [agility, intelligence & safety](/research/agility-intelligence-safety/), [aerial physical intelligence](/research/aerial-physical-intelligence/), and [multi-robot collaboration](/research/multi-robot-collaboration/) — and the work routinely goes from theory to hardware: if you join, you will fly real robots.

        **What you'll find here**

        - Research at the intersection of planning, control, perception, and learning for aerial robots, published in venues such as T-RO, RA-L, ICRA, and IROS.
        - A hands-on experimental culture — see our [flight videos](https://www.youtube.com/@ACPLab-wpi) and [publications](/publication/).
        - Mentorship aimed at making you an independent researcher, with regular one-on-one meetings and collaboration across the lab.

        ## Prospective PhD students

        Apply to the [WPI Robotics Engineering PhD program](https://www.wpi.edu/academics/departments/robotics-engineering) and name Prof. Guanrui Li as a faculty member you would like to work with. You are also welcome to email [Prof. Li](mailto:gli7@wpi.edu) directly — attach your **CV**, **transcripts**, and, if you have prior publications, a copy of your most significant work.

        One request: in your email, tell us which ACP Lab paper or video you found most interesting and why. Messages that do this are read first — it shows us you know what the lab actually works on.

        ## Postdocs and visiting scholars

        Email [Prof. Li](mailto:gli7@wpi.edu) with your CV, one or two representative publications, and a short note on your research interests and intended dates.

        ## Current WPI students

        For MS and undergraduate students interested in research or project work (including MQPs and directed research), please fill out the **[lab application form](https://forms.office.com/r/8j6a3X5Uqt)** and browse the open [student projects](/projects/) for topics.

        ---

        Due to the volume of inquiries we receive, we cannot reply to every message. If you have not heard back within two weeks, we were likely unable to consider your application this cycle — but applications through the official WPI PhD program are always reviewed.
